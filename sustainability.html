SOCIAL SUSTAINABILITY · PROPOSED BACKY REWARDS

Make safety progress rewarding for frontline workers.

Designed for lower-wage frontline teams, the proposed programme recognises improvement, not comparison. Each worker sets a high-risk posture baseline in week one, earns points for lowering risk and consistent wear, then receives monthly recognition and wellness credits. Team milestones create shared rewards. Individual posture scores stay private and are never used for discipline.

01 · BASELINESet each worker’s own starting point in week one
02 · IMPROVERecognise lower high-risk posture rates and consistent wear
03 · REWARDUnlock monthly wellness credits and shared team milestones
04 · SUSTAINUse quarterly goals to keep safer habits going

Corporate partners can work with TacnIQ.ai to shape a worker-first programme and evaluate the safety and wellbeing outcomes.
