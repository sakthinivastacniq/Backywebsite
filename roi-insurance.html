Corporate insurance · Compensation · Treatment

Know where the money really goes after a back injury.

Workplace back injuries may trigger treatment bills, employee absence, work injury compensation and higher insurance costs. This guide distinguishes the total cost of an injury from the avoidable cost retained by an employer—the amount that should enter Backy's financial ROI calculation.

Two documented Singapore work-related back-injury awards

S$26,200 WICA award: A worker injured his back handling an overhead aircraft compartment and underwent treatment including physiotherapy. This earlier work injury was documented in [2024] SGHC 249, paragraph 9. In the company calculator this amount can be used as a high-severity single-case sensitivity, never as a national average.

S$15,000 civil damages component: A factory worker suffered a lumbar disc injury while lifting and stacking industrial metal components. [2004] SGHC 100 records S$15,000 specifically for disc-related pain and suffering, separate from other awarded damage categories. This is not WICA compensation and cannot be combined with WICA without confirming the applicable legal route.

For context, MOM published a 2022 healthcare worker median of S$11,560 for lump-sum compensation involving permanent incapacity or death across all injury types. Neither this figure nor the cases above establishes typical employer-paid annual back-injury costs.

1 · Insurer-paid claims

Published claim costs reflect the burden on an insurance system. Unless the employer is self-insured, they do not directly become employer savings.

2 · Employer-funded costs

Paid lost time, overtime, replacement labour, deductibles, direct treatment and case management can enter the financial model if genuinely avoidable.

3 · Future insurance benefit

A premium discount is modeled only when documented by the insurer or broker. Otherwise set it to zero; premium volume alone does not prove savings.

Insurance and physiotherapy benchmarks by country

These values are specific published price examples, official system-wide statistics or identified proxies—not seven interchangeable national averages. Currency is local to each country.

🇸🇬 SingaporePremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

Published group-plan premium examples, not a market average: Income FlexCare primary-care S$252.88–313.92/employee/year; specialist rider S$144.97–222.36/employee/year. Liberty SMECare lists WICA manual-worker S$218/employee/year, but only for up to 25 employees and with occupation exclusions.

Claims / compensation severity

MOM 2025: S$135.5 million WICA compensation / 28,895 awarded claims ≈ S$4,689/claim, across all claim types and excluding medical expenses. S$53,000 is the current medical-expense cap, not average spend.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

Private follow-ups commonly S$131–210/session (2026 published-price survey). Six sessions ≈ S$786–1,260 total treatment bill, before reimbursement.

Who pays and what Backy can save

WICA and group medical policies can cover eligible claims. Only deductible, excluded treatment, paid absence and documented premium experience count as employer savings.

🇺🇸 United StatesPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

2024 national median workers’ compensation premium index: US$1.09 per US$100 payroll (1.09%), across modeled classifications. Not a quote for a warehouse.

Claims / compensation severity

NCCI's estimated 2025 lost-time case average: US$31,300 indemnity plus US$30,600 medical ≈ US$61,900 per lost-time claim; not specifically back strain.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

US$137 per hour-long session average self-pay treatment price (2026), before contracted insurance discounts.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Workers' comp carrier usually pays insured claims; experience modification, deductibles, self-insurance and guaranteed pricing adjustments determine corporate exposure.

🇦🇺 AustraliaPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

Workers’ compensation premiums are set by state/territory, business classification and claims history. There is no reliable single national employer rate for a 100-worker logistics site.

Claims / compensation severity

2024–25 serious body-stressing cases: 51,800. Median paid compensation A$21,200 and median 9.5 working weeks lost; neither is a mean back-specific claim.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

Queensland WorkCover's July 2026 subsequent physio consultation benchmark A$108; fee schedules differ by jurisdiction.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Statutory insurer usually funds treatment/claim benefits. Track actual employer excess, return-to-work burden, wages and renewal quote effects.

Sources: Safe Work Australia 2026 ↗ · WorkCover Queensland treatment fee update ↗

🇨🇦 CanadaPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

British Columbia WorkSafeBC 2026 average base rate C$1.55 per C$100 of assessable payroll (1.55%). This is BC, not a Canadian national premium and not an industry quote.

Claims / compensation severity

Provincial boards determine accepted injury and medical benefits. There is no defensible all-Canada back-injury claim average for use as an employer saving here.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

BC WorkSafeBC fee reference: C$95/physiotherapy visit effective April 2026, where the applicable schedule permits.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Compensation boards pay approved claims. Employer savings primarily follow documented experience-rated contributions, retained expenses and noninsured absence.

Sources: WorkSafeBC 2026 base rates ↗ · WorkSafeBC health care benefits ↗

🇬🇧 United KingdomPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

Employer’s Liability insurance is generally compulsory; premiums depend on payroll, sector, claims experience and insurer. No uniform national £/worker rate is published.

Claims / compensation severity

HSE provides employer-specific appraisal values for average work injuries and work-related illness; these are different from payout amounts and can be more relevant than insurer-paid claims.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

Private physiotherapy 2026 median: £74 initial assessment and £63 per follow-up, based on a clinic-owner survey.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Employer-paid sick pay, agency cover, productivity and employer-funded occupational health are the principal direct savings; a liability insurer's payout is not automatically saved cash.

Sources: HSE employer cost appraisal ↗ · UK clinic-owner physio benchmarks ↗

🇩🇪 GermanyPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

German Social Accident Insurance (DGUV) average employer accident-insurance contribution in 2024: 1.09% of gross payroll; occupational hazard classes differ.

Claims / compensation severity

Compulsory accident insurance generally bears occupational claim and approved rehab treatment, while employers have paid sick-leave and operational exposure.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

No robust nationwide private physiotherapy average validated here. Use the employer's actual occupational-health invoices; otherwise set employer-funded physio to €0.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Do not add social-insurance payments or aggregate production-loss estimates as if they were a cash saving. Employer-paid sick wages and replacement costs require site validation.

Sources: Germany Trade & Invest insurance overview ↗ · DGUV average accident contribution ↗

🇳🇱 NetherlandsPremiums · claims · physio ↘

Insurance premium benchmark

Commercial sickness-absence insurance brokerage benchmark: around 1.5%–5% of gross payroll; broker estimate, not official market-wide average. Employer sick-pay obligation generally at least 70% up to two years.

Claims / compensation severity

No directly comparable national workers' compensation claim amount; avoid importing US or Singapore claim severities.

Physiotherapy treatment costs

2026 private/self-funded physiotherapy commonly €35–45 per regular session; €45–55 examples from Dutch provider price lists.

Who pays and what Backy can save

Wage continuation, reintegration and case management are more directly relevant than insurer claims; employer-specific absence insurance and reimbursement determine net savings.

Both thresholds exclude any separate verified premium discount and assume no change in workforce, pricing or impact. They are mathematical targets, not known site-level costs.
