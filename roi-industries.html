THE FINANCIAL CONNECTION

From HRP improvement to avoided injury costs

Estimated claim / absence reduction = Reported or assumed HRP reduction × chosen conversion factor
Prevented relevant cases = Baseline cases × estimated reduction × years
Employer gross savings = avoidable absence + retained claim cost + incident administration + employer-funded nonclaim physio + confirmed premium credit
Net ROI = (gross employer savings − Backy deployment cost) ÷ Backy deployment cost × 100%

Published national premium benchmarks estimate what insurance might cost, not the discount a carrier will provide for Backy.
