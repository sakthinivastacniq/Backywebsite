Local reference: Insurance / workplace cost context ↗

Formula: Sector serious claims per million hours × 2,000 hours/worker/year × number of workers ÷ 1,000,000 = benchmark cases/year. We apply the published 35.2% national body-stressing share as a proxy for potentially relevant cases; this includes more than back injuries. The fixed 40% assumed conversion multiplies measured or hypothetical posture improvement to model injury change. Projected cases × industry median weeks lost × five workdays × assumed daily employer cost, plus fixed incident administration, yields modeled employer value. Backy cost includes one-time device and 36 months' software.

Unverified assumptions: 40% posture-to-injury conversion, 2,000 annual work hours, local operating day cost, administrative cost, exchange rates and commercial pricing. These inputs are locked for visitor simplicity, not because they are scientifically proven. Insurer-paid compensation and unconfirmed premium discounts are excluded.
