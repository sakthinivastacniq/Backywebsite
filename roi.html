What could safer movement save your business?

Backy is a lower-back wearable that uses real-time vibration cues to help workers recognise and adjust high-risk bending or lifting. Reducing this exposure may help prevent strain over time. Use this calculator to explore what fewer back-related absences and employer-paid injury costs could mean for your business.
