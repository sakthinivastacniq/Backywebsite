How we calculate ROI.

We estimate the potential economic value of safer movement across absenteeism, replacement labour, productivity, insurance and ergonomic assessments, then compare it with the cost of deploying Backy.

← Back to the Impact Explorer

Our approach

1. Estimate the cost exposure

Use workforce size, occupational risk, back-pain burden, local wages and insurance cost references.

2. Estimate recoverable value

Apply planning assumptions to estimate the potential reduction in each cost category, without counting the same loss twice.

3. Calculate the return

Compare combined potential benefits against device and software costs over the selected 1-, 3- or 5-year period.

The calculation

Absenteeism and replacement labour

Absence value = recovered uncovered hours × hourly wageReplacement savings = recovered covered hours × replacement hourly cost

Recovered absence is estimated from occupational injury exposure and additional back-pain absence, after removing overlapping hours. Each recovered hour belongs to only one of these two categories.

Productivity

Productivity value = estimated back-pain-related productivity hours lost while at work × recovery assumption × hourly wage

Excludes time already counted as absence.

Insurance

Potential premium savings = annual reference premium × eligible renewal years × targeted risk share × assumed injury reduction × premium pass-through assumption

Conditional savings start after the first year and depend on actual insurer terms. Insurer-paid compensation is excluded.

Ergonomic assessment

Assessment value = estimated analyst hours saved × hourly wage

Represents potential time saved on ergonomic reviews, not the removal of required assessments.

Combined return on investment

Combined benefit = absence value + replacement savings + productivity value + insurance savings + assessment valueBacky investment = equipped workers × (volume-tier device price + monthly software fee × months)

Bulk hardware pricing applies to all devices at the qualifying order size, assuming one device per employee. Where a price range applies, the estimate uses its upper end. Monthly software fees remain unchanged. Market currency conversions and final quotations may vary.

Net benefit = combined benefit − Backy investmentROI (%) = net benefit ÷ Backy investment × 100

These are illustrative economic estimates, not measured or guaranteed Backy financial outcomes. The model uses Australian occupational risk benchmarks across markets, local wage and insurance references, and assumed recovery rates. Actual injury reductions, productivity gains, premium changes and assessment savings require customer validation. Labour value may not translate directly into cash savings. Pricing and workforce are assumed constant over the selected period.
